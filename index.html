How these work

Until slot 4 has a line worth locking, only very high rolls elsewhere earn a lock, because every lock makes each further try at slot 4 cost more. Each such lock lowers what slot 4 has to roll. Once slot 4 is locked, the other slots drop to their lower bar. A line you already have locked that is within the last number of points of its bar is left alone and shown as "okay". "Lock" means a line clears the top bar; "decent lock" means it clears only the current one, so it is your call.

Roll tracker

Per board and how cost is worked out

Each counted roll is priced by how many lines were locked at the time: 36, 40, 44, 60, 96, 172, 248, 324, 400 Soul Crystals for 0 to 8 locks, and 250 Kina per crystal. The figures for 1 and 8 locks are estimates, so totals that include them are marked with ~. Rolls are only counted while sharing the screen.

A line is worth locking when its stat is one you picked for that slot and it rolled at least the slot's current bar.
